THE IDEA

Knowing something is one thing.
Helping someone else see it is another.

I enjoy the moment when an abstract idea stops feeling abstract. Whether it is code, a circuit or a chess position, my goal is to make the underlying idea visible enough for someone else to take ownership of it.
